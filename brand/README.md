# Lamdis mark

A denial letter with a check seal, on amber. The app's promise is that every
fact in the appeal is checked against the person's own papers.

- `lamdis-icon.svg`: the full mark, a 1024 square. The iPhone icon, the
  apple-touch icon and anything 48px or larger.
- `lamdis-mark-small.svg`: a 32 tile with heavier lines and two text rules,
  for 32px and under (favicons, header marks).

Colors: amber `#FFB22E`, ink `#1A1814`, paper `#FFFFFF`.
